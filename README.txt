MaoMao — your apothecary desktop companion
==========================================

A tiny, fan-made MaoMao (The Apothecary Diaries) who lives on your Windows desktop:
she walks on your taskbar and windows, gathers herbs, grinds medicine, reads,
taste-tests poison, naps, keeps your to-do list, reminds you to take breaks,
and chats with you — in English, Uzbek, or whatever language you write in.

HOW TO START
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. Documents\MaoMao).
2. Double-click MaoMao.exe.
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed.
   Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
3. A welcome window will help you connect a FREE Google Gemini key so she can talk
   (aistudio.google.com/apikey -> "Create API key" -> paste it). You can skip this
   and add it later: right-click MaoMao -> Settings.

CONTROLS
- Click MaoMao — talk to her
- Drag — pick her up (throw her and she gets angry)
- Right-click — menu: tasks, settings, sleep, hide, quit
- Desktop shortcut / Ctrl+Alt+M — turn her on/off

THINGS TO SAY
- "Remind me to call mom tomorrow at 6 pm"
- "Every day at 9 remind me to take my vitamins"
- "What are my tasks?"   /   "I called mom" (marks it done)
- Ask about health, cooking, code, or anything else.

PRIVACY
Your conversations, tasks and settings are stored only on your computer
(%APPDATA%\MaoMao). Messages are sent only to the AI service you choose
(Google Gemini / Anthropic). API keys are encrypted with Windows (DPAPI).

UNINSTALL
Right-click -> Settings -> General -> untick "Start with Windows", quit,
then delete this folder and %APPDATA%\MaoMao.
